Labour Unit

Four rooms, two on each side of the container, each with a single bunk bed for two people, a tall cupboard, a small kitchenette and its own door. Each pair of rooms shares a bathroom with a toilet and shower in between, closed off by a canvas curtain. Meals can be added at an extra cost.
