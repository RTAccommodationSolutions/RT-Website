What we do

We deliver portable container housing for employees, set up wherever your project is in Southern Africa and looked after for as long as it runs.

Four unit types

From a private room with its own bathroom to a twelve-sleeper with a shared kitchen, so every member of the team has the right space.

Meals and laundry

A continental breakfast, a packed lunch and a three-course dinner every day, plus a laundry service, on our top two tiers.

A well-run site

Our own apps track every clean, every laundry basket and every item in the stock room, so problems get sorted quickly.
