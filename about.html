About RT Accommodation Solutions

RT Accommodation Solutions delivers portable container housing for employees working away from home.

We can set up a site at any location in Southern Africa, close to where your project is, and we maintain and manage the containers for the full duration of the project.

RT was founded in 2023 in Kouga, Eastern Cape. Since then the company has serviced many clients in various industries. Both locally and at a national scale. With future plans for expansion.
