    Common questions

    What's included with each unit?

    Every unit has air conditioning. The Manager and Skilled Labour units also include three meals a day, wifi and a laundry service. The Labour and Weekly Worker units don't include meals, but they can be added at an extra cost.

    What meals do you serve?

    A continental breakfast, a packed lunch to take to site and a three-course dinner, every day.

    What if we don't take meals?

    Teams in the Labour and Weekly Worker units who don't add meals can cook for themselves in the kitchen provided.

    Can we mix unit types?

    Answer to come. For example: yes, most teams book a Manager Unit alongside Labour units.
